Juno Editor

A precise preset editor and librarian for Roland Juno-106 and MKS-7 synthesizers. Connect via MIDI to save, organize, and edit presets with effortless control.

Presets as Files

Store presets as .jep documents or .syx sysex files on your drive. Ideal for backups, sharing, and version control without relying on synth memory.

Keyboard Playback

Play and audition presets directly from your Mac keyboard. Test changes in real-time with responsive, low-latency performance.

Undo and Search

Full undo history keeps your edits safe. Quick search finds any preset instantly across your library.

Retina-Ready Interface

Crisp, high-resolution design optimized for modern Mac displays. Customizable MIDI out and channel settings right in the main window.

Seamless Compatibility

Works flawlessly with both Juno-106 and MKS-7. Version 2.8.3 brings refined fader animations, classic color tweaks, updated toolbar, and manual mode options.

How It Works

1. Connect

Link your Juno-106 or MKS-7 via MIDI. Set output channel directly in the app.

2. Load or Create

Pull presets from synth memory or build from scratch using intuitive controls.

3. Edit and Play

Tweak parameters, audition with keyboard playback, and undo as needed.

4. Save and Organize

Export as .jep or .syx files. Search, tag, and manage your library effortlessly.
